# KIAAN HRM - COMPANY POLICY HANDBOOK (KNOWLEDGE BASE)

## 1. Attendance Policy (हाजिरी नियम)
* Working Hours: Standard check-in time is between 09:00 AM and 09:30 AM.
* Late Check-in: Checking in after 09:30 AM is marked as "Late". Repeated lateness (more than 3 times in a month) may trigger automatic salary deductions based on company settings.
* Absenteeism: Not checking in by 12:00 PM without prior approved leave marks the day as "Absent".

## 2. Leave Policy (छुट्टी के नियम)
* Application Process: Employees can directly apply for leave (Annual Leave, Sick Leave, Emergency Leave, Unpaid Leave) from their portal.
* Admin Approval: There are no fixed hardcoded leave balance caps. Every leave request is reviewed and approved or rejected by the Company Admin/Manager.
* Advance Notice: Leave requests should be submitted at least 24 hours in advance whenever possible for timely manager approval.
* Medical Certificate: For extended sick leaves (more than 2 consecutive days), HR or Admin may request a medical certificate upon approval.

## 3. Expense Claims & Reimbursements (खर्चे का क्लेम)
* Eligible Claims: Travel, client entertainment, office supplies, and training/certifications.
* Submission Limit: Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date along with proof/receipts.
* Approval & Credit: Claims are reviewed and approved by Company HR/Admin. Approved reimbursements are credited along with monthly payroll.

## 4. Shift & Work Location (लोकेशन नियम)
* Geofencing: Employees must punch in/out from within the company's designated geofence radius. Punching outside the geofence will be blocked or flagged with an alert.
* Shift Assignment: Shift schedules are assigned by the company manager.
